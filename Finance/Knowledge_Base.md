# Finance — Knowledge Base: Consulting FP&A

**Maintained by:** Finance · **Last updated:** July 3, 2026 · Living document

## 1. Overview & why it matters for Semantic Arts

For a consulting firm, financial health lives in a handful of operating metrics — utilization, realization, project margin, and revenue per consultant — plus disciplined cash management. This knowledge base captures the current benchmarks and the reporting rhythm that keeps a professional-services firm profitable.

## 2. Current landscape & key trends (2025–2026)

Published professional-services benchmarks:

- **Utilization:** poor <60%, median 65–72%, best >78%; firms generally need ~75%+ to be comfortably profitable.
- **Realization** (billed vs. standard rate): poor <85%, median 88–93%, best >95%.
- **Project margin:** poor <25%, median 30–38%, best >45%; firm-wide operating margins typically run **15–30%**.
- **Revenue per consultant:** poor <$150K, median $180–250K, best >$300K.

Sources: [Ruddr](https://www.ruddr.com/post/the-15-most-important-kpis-for-consulting-firms), [Rocketlane](https://www.rocketlane.com/blogs/professional-services-kpis), [Mosaic](https://www.mosaicapp.com/post/consulting-firm-profitability-benchmarks-you-need-to-know). A notable 2026 trend: generative AI is projected to lift **effective utilization by 10–15 percentage points** by automating research, documentation, and analysis. Recommended cadence is a **weekly operational review** (utilization forecast, milestone completion) and a **monthly financial review** (project margin, revenue per consultant, realization) ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/professional-services-growth/professional-services-metrics)).

## 3. Best practices

Track leading indicators (utilization forecast, pipeline coverage) weekly and lagging financials monthly. Watch the gap between effective and average bill rate — persistent discounts or over-servicing quietly erode margin. Tie every model to explicit assumptions and cite the source of each figure. Keep a rolling cash-flow projection, not just a backward-looking P&L.

## 4. Application to Semantic Arts

Semantic Arts is a privately held **S-corporation** with a Governance Committee, trustees, and quarterly trust distributions/stipends — so reporting obligations (monthly close, trustee financial reports, quarterly GC reporting) are a real cadence, already reflected in the Company Calendar. Its consultant-pay model is billing-rate-and-chargeability based, which makes **utilization and realization the metrics that most directly drive both firm margin and individual pay**. As a boutique, it can command premium rates, so realization discipline matters more than chasing volume. Finance currently works from exports (connector-free).

## 5. Recommendations / opportunities

Stand up a simple monthly KPI dashboard (utilization, realization, project margin, revenue per consultant) benchmarked against the ranges above. Build a reusable rate/utilization/margin model so pricing and hiring decisions can be tested quickly. Track the AI-utilization uplift as staff adopt Claude — it should show up as higher effective utilization. Keep owner distributions analyzed separately from wage/comp benchmarks.
