# Knowledge Base — Direct-Report 1:1s

**Maintained by:** Otto (for the COO) · **Living document** · **Last updated:** 2026-07-07
**Related:** Meeting Intelligence KB; TASKS.md (task board); Company Calendar; Operations Manager Living JD; Marketing KB.

## Purpose

A running record of the COO's one-on-ones with the two direct reports, **Lucas (Growth Marketing Manager)** and **Amanda (Operations Manager)**. For each person it tracks **decisions made** and **outstanding tasks / open items**, plus anything that should become a calendar entry.

## How to use it

- Tell Otto the person and the notes and Otto logs them under that person with the date.
- Ask things like "what's outstanding with Lucas," "what did I decide with Amanda," or "pull open items from my last 1:1s."
- Otto may proactively flag an open item as a candidate task or calendar entry, but **always asks before adding anything** to the task board or calendar. Suggested disposition is noted in the "Suggested next step" column; the COO confirms.

## Fast capture (paste format)

Paste raw notes in this shape and Otto files them automatically. Messy bullets are fine; the labels just speed the sort.

```
Person: <Lucas | Amanda>, <date>
Decisions: <one per line, or separated by ;>
Open: <open items / tasks; note status if known>
Calendar: <anything with a date>
```

Otto sorts Decisions and Open items into the tables (with the date) and flags anything under Calendar as a proposed entry, confirming before adding.

## Log format (per person)

- **Decisions** table: Date · Decision · Notes.
- **Open items** table: Date raised · Item / task · Status · Suggested next step (task / calendar / track).
