# Knowledge Base — Meeting Intelligence

**Maintained by:** Otto (for the COO) · **Living document** · **Last updated:** 2026-07-07
**Related:** Company Calendar (meeting cadences); TASKS.md (task board); Operations Playbook.

## Purpose

A running record of what actually gets decided and left open in the firm's standing meetings. For each meeting it tracks two things: **decisions made** and **outstanding questions / open items**. Use it to:

- surface the decisions made in a meeting (or across meetings) for reporting;
- surface anything still outstanding and, where appropriate, move it to the task board or the Company Calendar;
- let Otto proactively suggest which open items should become tasks or calendar entries.

## How to use it

- Tell Otto the meeting and the notes (decisions, open questions, who owns them) and Otto logs them in the right section with the date.
- Ask things like "what did we decide in the exec meeting," "what's outstanding from the sales meeting," or "turn the open items from Friday staff into tasks."
- Otto can suggest dispositions for open items (task board, calendar, or just track), but stays advisory: the COO confirms before anything is created. Suggested disposition is noted in the "Suggested next step" column.

## Fast capture (paste format)

Paste raw notes in this shape and Otto files them automatically. Messy bullets are fine; the labels just speed the sort.

```
Meeting: <name>, <date>
Decisions: <one per line, or separated by ;>
Open: <open items / questions; note owner if known>
Calendar: <anything with a date>
```

Otto sorts Decisions and Open items into the tables (with the date) and flags anything under Calendar as a proposed entry, confirming before adding.

## Log format (per meeting)

- **Decisions** table: Date · Decision · Owner / notes.
- **Open items** table: Date raised · Item / question · Owner · Status · Suggested next step (task / calendar / track).
