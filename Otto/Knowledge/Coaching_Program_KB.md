# Knowledge Base — Coaching Program

**Maintained by:** Otto (for the COO) · **Living document** · **Last updated:** 2026-07-07
**Related:** Operations Playbook (SOP: Coaching program); Company Calendar (monthly coaches meeting); agenda archive `Coaches_Meeting_Agendas.docx` (this folder).

## Overview

Semantic Arts runs an internal coaching program: experienced staff coach colleagues (coachees) to support development and retention. The program is chaired and managed by the COO, who contributes but is **not a coach**. The President and CEO attend meetings but **do not coach directly**.

## Roles

- **COO (JT)** — manages the program: runs the monthly meeting, sets the agenda, assigns coaches to coachees, rotates who brings discussion questions, sends announcements, and tracks action items. Contributes to discussion but does not coach.
- **President & CEO** — attend the coaches meeting; do not coach directly.
- **Coaches** — carry coachees and meet monthly as a group. Roster as of mid-2026 has included Dylan, Steven, Jamie, Dalia, Doug, and Rebecca, with Dan transitioning out (Steven brought in as a replacement). *Confirm the current roster before relying on it; it changes.*

## Cadence

- **Coaches meeting:** monthly, the **3rd Friday** of each month.
- **Coaching sessions:** standardized to occur in **March, June, September, and December**.

## Meeting agenda template

1. **Open & coach accountability** — each coach: are you on track with your coaching sessions?
2. **(30m) Open discussion** — coaches bring challenges, thoughts, or ideas from their sessions.
3. **(15m) Question discussion** — 2 questions for the group to discuss and learn from; a designated coach brings them (rotating responsibility).
4. **(15m) Book discussion** — one chapter from the current reading.
5. **Close** — commitments, accountabilities, and action items.

## Current reading

**"The Coaching Habit"** (Michael Bungay Stanier). The group is just starting it (Intro and Chapter 1 first).

## Standing admin notes

- Training-budget requests are handled case-by-case; coaches direct staff to fill out the request form.
- The COO assigns new coachees and sends the announcement when a coach or coachee changes.
- Meeting agendas are archived in `Coaches_Meeting_Agendas.docx` (this folder); new agendas can be generated from the template above.
