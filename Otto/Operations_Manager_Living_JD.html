Role summary

The Operations Manager keeps the firm's financial-operations rhythm running: expense and payroll support, accounts-payable processing, trustee and governance financial preparation, and client-satisfaction check-ins. The role is the COO's operational right hand for recurring execution.

Duties by area

Finance & accounts payable (support to COO) - Receives from JT and processes, monthly: the Key Bank line-of-credit payment amount (15th), the Canada bank statement (24th), and the Microsoft invoices (24th). (Calendar: monthly) - Records trust and trustee payments in QuickBooks. (Task; Governance: Trust interaction)

Payroll & expense-reimbursement support - Enters all approved Expensify expense reports into the expense spreadsheet on the Wednesday before the pay period ends. (Playbook: Expense reimbursement; Calendar) - Moves the approved expenses into the payroll spreadsheet at the Friday payroll run so they are reimbursed with payroll. (Playbook: Expense reimbursement; Calendar)

Governance & trustee support - Prepares the quarterly trustee-meeting financials at the start of each quarter for the COO to review, which are then shared with the Governance Committee. (Governance: Trust interaction; Calendar) - Prepares the quarterly GC financial report. (Calendar: quarterly) - Prepares the monthly trustee financial reports (1st of month). (Calendar — confirm this sits with the Ops Manager)

Client success - Runs the mid-contract client-satisfaction check-in (keep / stop / start) with sponsors using the standard check-in template. (Playbook: Mid-contract client satisfaction check-in — [ACTIVE]) - On the 1st of each month, reviews which clients are due for a 1:1 satisfaction check-in and flags contracts near their midpoint, feeding the COO's monthly coordination. (Calendar: monthly)

Recurring cadence

  • Per pay cycle (every 4 weeks): expense entry (Wednesday before period end); move expenses to payroll spreadsheet (Friday run).
  • Monthly: process LOC payment (15th), Canada statement and Microsoft invoices (24th), trustee financial reports (1st), client check-in review (1st).
  • Quarterly: prepare trustee-meeting financials (start of quarter), GC financial report, record trust/trustee payments in QB.

Systems used

QuickBooks, Expensify, the payroll spreadsheet, the cash plan, and Microsoft 365.
