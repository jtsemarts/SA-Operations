## Role summary

The COO runs operations, HR, marketing operations, sales logistics, most of finance operations, IT and security, compliance, facilities, vendor management, and all financial and operational reporting. Approves single financial commitments under $2,500 (jointly with the President at $2,500 and above). Reports to the President; owns firm operating policy and the recurring operating rhythm.

**No separate IT or compliance department:** the COO is the acting IT and compliance function and performs this work directly. "Delegating to IT" is not available; the levers for IT and compliance load are automation, vendor support, pushing mechanical steps to the Operations Manager (Amanda), and AI/agent assistance (with Legal for issue-spotting).

## Duties by function

**Finance & accounting**
- Day-to-day cash management and use of the line of credit; process remittances; project future sources and uses of cash and plan accordingly.
- Subsidiary (Canada) accounting; bills, payables, purchasing, and bookkeeping; set firm financial-management policies.
- Payroll processing (President approves). Payroll runs every 4 weeks (13 periods/year); workweek Friday→Thursday. Approve Expensify reports the Monday of the run week; run payroll the following Friday; finishing tasks the Monday after. At each payroll, accrue 3% of gross earned revenue as the trust contribution.
- Monthly close and cash-flow projection; AR and invoicing; time tracking and billing; project profitability and utilization; budgeting and spending-vs-budget monitoring.
- Tax and compliance coordination with the firm accountant; IRS and tax reporting; annual audit/tax-prep coordination.
- Records retention (owns the records-retention policy).

**Governance & trust**
- Administers Governance Committee elections; maintains the decision/authority matrix.
- Administers trust interaction: 3% of gross earned revenue accrued each payroll and remitted quarterly via KeyNavigator; $500 quarterly stipend per trustee (Abhishek held); reviews Amanda's quarterly trustee-meeting financials and shares them to the GC; participates in the standing quarterly GC–trustee meeting. Pays distributions.
- Delivers quarterly financial and operational reporting to the Governance Committee.

**Human resources**
- Manages the recruiting and interview process; prepares and manages offers (President signs); onboarding administration; offboarding.
- Performance-review administration and 360 feedback; compensation reviews and salary surveys; PTO/leave administration (unlimited PTO); benefits administration and open enrollment.
- Multi-state employment compliance; contractor/employee classification; training-budget administration (delegated for non-technical staff); chairs the coaching program; employee-handbook and personnel-file maintenance.

**Marketing operations**
- Oversight of the website, campaigns, DCAF and EDTS, and marketing budget priorities; functional oversight of the marketing team and spend; metrics and alignment to strategy.

**Sales logistics & partnerships**
- Administrative oversight of sales logistics (setting up client calls) and sales-team management; pipeline tracking/reporting support.
- Federal-sales research; partnership development (AIM partnership — "The Connected Customer", plus the pharma clinical-trial offering).

**IT & security**
- Account provisioning/deprovisioning; SaaS and tool inventory; equipment and device management; data backup and retention (including the monthly SharePoint backup); IT security and incident response; acceptable-use policy.

**Compliance & legal (operational)**
- Contract intake and records; NDA handling; data protection; multi-state and international compliance monitoring; records retention and legal-hold coordination; regulatory and filing tracking. Ensures adherence to the AI Usage Policy.

**Facilities & administration**
- Vendor and contract management; insurance review and renewals; travel and expense policy; procurement; facilities and lease management; Team Summit logistics.

**Reporting & analytics**
- Owns all financial and operational reporting and defines the metrics. Maintains the weekly Financial Evaluation Dashboard (cash on hand, rolling 4-week average, weekly margin vs target, renewal and new-work pipeline, overdue/total AR, and the cash-trajectory-vs-buffer health view).

## Recurring cadence

- **Weekly (Monday-centered):** update the cash & staff plan; AR schedule; archive the cash plan; evaluation spreadsheet; QuickBooks export (Thursday); pull the Monday cash balance for the dashboard.
- **Per pay cycle (every 4 weeks):** approve Expensify (Monday of run week), run payroll (Friday), finishing tasks (Monday after); accrue the 3% trust contribution.
- **Monthly:** LOC payment amount to Amanda (15th); review upcoming monthly invoices (25th); MS invoices and Canada statement to Amanda (24th); SharePoint backup (28th).
- **Quarterly:** trust remittance and trustee stipends; GC financial and operational reporting; standing GC–trustee meeting; Form 941; estimated tax payments; Operations Playbook and charter review.
- **Annual:** salary-survey and job-description review; insurance audit; W-2s/1099s; Spark position/rate updates; budget setting.
