# Semantic Arts — Voice Profile

**Maintained by:** Brand · **Status:** aligned to the SA22 Brand Kit v3.1 (July 2026)

See `Brand_Agent_Charter.md` and `Semantic_Arts_Brand_Kit.md`. Voice is the constant personality; tone flexes by context. This profile applies to all content, external and internal.

**Platform message:** "One elegant data model can change everything."
**Audience:** Chief Data Officers and senior data leaders who are frustrated that data initiatives are fragmented and reactive, like pushing a boulder uphill. We name that struggle with empathy, then offer the proven path out of it. Clients are the heroes, cast as "Data Masters".

## The four voice pillars

1. **Authoritative.** We are fully specialized in data-centric transformation. We speak with earned expertise, never arrogance and never hedging.
2. **Empathetic.** We name the CDO's frustration (fragmented initiatives, firefighting, throwing good money after bad), then offer the way through.
3. **Principled.** The three promises are brand identity, not fine print: No Contract Bloat. No Change Orders. No Lock-in. Keep them front and center.
4. **Clear.** Simplicity is the product. If a sentence needs a second read, rewrite it. The brand should embody what it sells.

## Voice dimensions

| Dimension | Position |
|---|---|
| Formal ↔ Casual | Formal-professional |
| Serious ↔ Funny | Serious, occasional dry wit |
| Respectful ↔ Irreverent | Respectful |
| Matter-of-fact ↔ Enthusiastic | Matter-of-fact, with conviction |

## Write like this

- Specific and empathetic: "We know how overwhelming it can be to give your heart and soul to transforming data and still feel like you're pushing a boulder uphill."
- Bold and clear: "One elegant data model can change everything."
- Concrete outcomes: "All new information-intensive initiatives, 10x easier."
- Name the culprit directly: "Application-centric thinking, pushed by self-serving vendors."
- Lead with the promises: "No Contract Bloat. No Change Orders. No Platform Lock-in."
- Frame clients as heroes: CDOs become "Data Masters".

## Anti-tone (avoid)

- Buzzword pile-up: "Leverage synergistic data ecosystems for transformational outcomes."
- Vague benefit claims: "We help you make better decisions with your data."
- Vendor bragging: "industry-leading, best-in-class, cutting-edge."
- Passive or hedging language: "We can help you potentially improve data access."
- Consumer or startup tone: casual, emoji-heavy, hype-driven.
- Lock-in language: anything that implies dependency or long commitment.
