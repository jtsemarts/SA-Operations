## The three writing rules

1. **No em dashes unless genuinely required.** Use a period, comma, parentheses, or "and"/"but". Avoid the en dash as a substitute too.
2. **Keep language simple and plain.** Short sentences; common words over jargon. Simplicity is the product; if a sentence needs a second read, rewrite it.
3. **Provide context and substantiation for every claim.** A reason, an example, or a source.

## Voice in brief (full profile in `semanticarts-voice-profile.md`)

- **Platform message:** "One elegant data model can change everything."
- **Four pillars:** Authoritative, Empathetic, Principled, Clear.
- **The three promises (keep front and center):** No Contract Bloat. No Change Orders. No Lock-in.
- **Frame clients as heroes** ("Data Masters"); name the culprit ("application-centric thinking"); avoid buzzwords, hedging, vendor bragging, and lock-in language.

## QC checklist (run on every draft before it reaches the COO)

**Check the canonical sources first.** For external or voice-heavy content, review the marketing vault's `SA-Voice-and-Messaging-Guide.md` and `Copyediting-and-Number-Style-Conventions.md` (read-only at `~/git/sa-marketing-os/50-Resources`) and align terminology, banned/filler words, and number style to them before running the checks below.

**Writing**
1. **Em dashes:** none, unless genuinely required; no en dash as a substitute.
2. **Plain language:** short sentences, common words, no filler.
3. **Claims:** each carries a reason, an example, or a source.
4. **Terminology and capitalization:** matches the tables below.
5. **Voice:** on profile; three promises present where relevant; no anti-tone language.

**Visual / brand (vital for external, apply the spirit internally)**
6. **Color:** Semantic Blue `#005A7D` and Semantic Gray `#808080` anchor; Burnt Orange `#C84A2B` for a single CTA per view only; Olive `#8C9E38` for kickers/active; Warm Gold and Pale Teal as accents. No off-palette colors.
7. **Logo:** correct SA22 variant for the background (Color on white, Black on light, White on dark); clear space kept; wordmark always lowercase "semantic arts"; never gray, never on gradients/orange.
8. **Typography:** Open Sans throughout; heading/body hierarchy per the brand kit; kicker labels uppercase, tracked, Olive.
9. **Imagery:** cool-toned, expert-in-context, holographic/structured data visuals; line icons (1.5–2px). No generic stock (handshakes, lightbulbs, puzzle pieces), no warm casts.

Return a short result: a clear "pass", or a "fix list" naming what changed and why.

## Capitalization conventions

- **The logo wordmark is always lowercase, "semantic arts."** In running body copy the company name is capitalized, "Semantic Arts."
- Product and ontology names keep their own casing wherever they appear. Write "gist" in lowercase even at the start of a sentence; recast the sentence rather than capitalizing it. Keep the internal capital in "AllegroGraph".
- Capitalize a job title when it names a specific office (Chief Operating Officer, President, CEO); lowercase a generic reference.
- Headings use sentence case unless an approved sample shows otherwise.

## Preferred terms / avoid terms

| Prefer | Avoid | Note |
|---|---|---|
| semantic arts (logo) / Semantic Arts (body) | SemanticArts, "SA" in outward copy | Wordmark lowercase; capitalized in prose. "the firm" or "we" on later reference. |
| data-centric | data centric, Data-Centric | Hyphenated, lowercase. "the data-centric revolution/movement". |
| application-centric | app-centric | Used to name the culprit we contrast against. |
| gist | Gist, GIST | The upper ontology. Lowercase always, even at sentence start. |
| knowledge graph (noun) / knowledge-graph (modifier) | knowledgegraph, Knowledge Graph | Two words; hyphenate only as an adjective. |
| ontology, ontologies | Ontology (mid-sentence) | Lowercase unless it begins a sentence. |
| AllegroGraph | Allegrograph, Allegro Graph | Keep the internal capital G. |
| Data Masters | (client-as-hero framing) | Cast CDOs/clients as the heroes. |
| No Contract Bloat / No Change Orders / No Lock-in | soft or hedged versions | The three promises; keep exact and prominent. |
