## Goal

> The AI & Cyber Task Force researches, discusses, and recommends ways for Semantic Arts to innovate with AI and technology while continually strengthening its security posture.

## Purpose

The working record for the firm's AI & Cyber Task Force. Otto uses it three ways: **generate the agenda** before each meeting, **log updates and recommendations** from each meeting, and **track action items** to closure.

## Authority

This is an **advisory group**. It brings ideas, does research, discusses, and makes **recommendations**. It does **not** hold decision authority. Final decisions rest with the **President** (who owns the AI policy) for most matters, and with the **COO** for internal infrastructure. The group's job is to develop well-researched recommendations and route them to the right decision-maker. As part of its mandate, the group also helps **draft and refine relevant policies** (for example VPN / remote access and password management), which the executive team approves.

## How Otto helps

- **Before a meeting:** Otto drafts an agenda from the standing template below, pre-filled with any open action items due and any updates logged since the last meeting. The chair edits and sends.
- **After a meeting:** tell Otto the notes (updates, recommendations, new action items and owners) and Otto logs them in a dated entry and updates the action-item tracker.
- **Between meetings:** open action items surface in the weekly rollup and the daily list; Otto proposes which should also become board tasks or calendar entries, and confirms before creating anything (cross-linked per the Knowledge Hub rules).

## Standing agenda template

This is the format to use for every meeting agenda going forward.

**Header block:** title "AI & Cyber Task Force"; "Meeting Agenda"; the date with "(monthly, 3rd Thursday)"; the "Chair: JT · Members: Rebecca, Lucas, Neil, Brett, Steven, Jamie" line; and the Goal statement.

1. **Welcome.**
2. **Action items from last meeting.**
   - Cover any questions on SharePoint, Teams, or other operational notes.
   - Discuss any decisions the executive team has made since the last meeting (VPN, password manager, and similar).
   - Any other follow-ups from the prior meeting's action items.
3. **New topics.** For each: options, discussion, and a recommendation with an owner and a next step. Final decisions are made by the executive team. Standing items:
   - Discussion of the AI policy: suggestions for changes or clarifications.
   - MCP and third-party connector utilization.
   - Potential presentation at the Monthly Company Update: topic, scope, and who presents.
   - Plus any new topics raised for this meeting.
4. **Action items and owners.**
5. **Next meeting (3rd Thursday of the next month) and close.**
