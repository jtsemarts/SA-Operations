## Overview

A reference hub for client relationships, satisfaction, and account context. Light for now; to be built out as client detail is added.

## Relationship ownership (from the governance model)

- **CEO** maintains the most senior relationships (for example Devon at Broadridge, Shawn at IDA, Ken at Omatic).
- **President** knows all sponsors, handles contentious issues and final negotiation, and administers NPS (Net Promoter Score).
- **COO** covers client legal/compliance needs, onboarding administration, invoicing, and approvals.
- **Operations Manager (Amanda)** runs the mid-contract 1:1 satisfaction check-ins and flags contracts near their midpoint (see the playbook SOP).
