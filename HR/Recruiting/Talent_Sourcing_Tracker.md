# Semantic Arts — Talent Sourcing Tracker (Ontology / Knowledge-Graph)

**Maintained by:** Otto · **Updated:** weekly by the scheduled sourcing pass, plus ad hoc
**Purpose:** A running list of US-based practitioners doing ontology or knowledge-graph / graph-engineering work, identified from public sources, for future pipeline building.

## Ground rules

- **Public professional information only.** Name, current employer, job title, and the public source. No private contact details (personal email, phone, home address).
- **US-based only.** Where US location is inferred rather than confirmed, mark it as "likely."
- **Not filtered by seniority** — capture anyone doing the work; career stage is a note, not a gate.
- **Verify before any outreach.** Current employer and availability change; confirm at the time of contact. No outreach happens without JT's approval.
- Scaled collection or storage of candidate data should be reviewed with Legal (privacy, GDPR for non-US, source terms of service). See the Candidate Sourcing SOP in the Operations Playbook.

## Sources reviewed each pass

Conference speakers (KGC, ISWC, ESWC); authors/publications (semantic-web venues, books, blogs); open-source contributors (RDFLib, LinkML, OBO Foundry, Protégé, kg-construct); company team pages (competitors and KG vendors); personnel-move news; W3C community group; Semantic Web Journal; Lotico; Code4Lib.
