# Chief Operating Officer (COO) — Job Description

**Company:** Semantic Arts, Inc. · **Location:** Fort Collins, Colorado (hybrid; supports a distributed, multi-state team)
**Reports to:** President · **Also:** Non-voting member of the Governance Committee
**Status:** Full-time, exempt · **Prepared:** July 2026

---

## About Semantic Arts

Semantic Arts is a ~30-person specialist consulting firm in data, ontology, and knowledge-graph technology, and the creator of the *gist* upper ontology. We are recognized thought leaders in the data-centric movement and work with senior, technical buyers at enterprises across the US and abroad. We are a privately held S-corporation with an emerging Governance Committee and trustee structure.

## Role summary

The COO drives Semantic Arts' internal operating functions and turns strategy into dependable execution. Reporting to the President, the COO owns the day-to-day management and outcomes of operations, human resources, finance operations, marketing operations, sales logistics, IT and security, compliance and risk, vendor management, and facilities. The COO has real authority to decide within defined thresholds and to direct the teams and committees that run these functions, while escalating the largest and least-reversible matters to the President and CEO. This is a builder's role: the firm is formalizing its operating model, and the COO is central to making leadership durable and the business run smoothly.

## Key responsibilities

**Operations leadership.** Own the roadmap, day-to-day management, and outcomes across the firm's internal functions. Establish and maintain the operating system of the firm (standard procedures, the operating calendar, and reporting) so nothing falls through the cracks.

**Finance operations.** Run day-to-day cash management and use of the line of credit, bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, and monthly close and cash-flow projection. Prepare all financial and operational reporting, including monthly trustee reporting and quarterly Governance Committee packs. Process payroll (approved by the President) and owner distributions. Monitor spending against budget. Coordinate tax, IRS, government, and jurisdictional reporting with the firm's accountant. Hold approval authority for single financial commitments under $2,500 (except the training budget), and jointly approve commitments of $2,500 and above with the President.

**Human resources.** Manage the full HR function: recruiting and interviewing, onboarding and offboarding, performance-review administration and 360 feedback, the coaching program, benefits administration, multi-state employment compliance, and personnel records. Partner with the President, who holds final decision authority on hiring, firing, promotion, and pay rates.

**Marketing and sales operations.** Provide functional oversight of the marketing team, budget, campaigns, website, and metrics with day-to-day management authority. Manage sales logistics and the sales team, supporting the President's ownership of proposals and customer contracts.

**IT, security, and risk.** Oversee IT and infrastructure, cybersecurity, data privacy, access provisioning and deprovisioning, the tool and SaaS inventory, and adequate insurance coverage. Maintain vendor and contract management and internal policies.

**Client operations.** Handle onboarding administration, invoicing and approvals, and the legal and compliance needs of client engagements, supporting the President's ownership of client delivery and satisfaction.

**Governance support.** Serve as a non-voting member of the Governance Committee, administer its elections, and report financials and operational metrics to the committee and trustees at regular intervals.

## Decision authority

- Approves any single financial commitment under $2,500 (except the training budget); may delegate approval of smaller amounts.
- Jointly approves expenditures and liabilities of $2,500 and above with the President.
- Holds day-to-day cash and line-of-credit management and view access to bank accounts (the President holds signing authority).
- Decides within functional thresholds and directs the teams and committees running each function; escalates matters above the thresholds, or those that are hard to reverse, to the President and CEO.

## Qualifications

- Progressive operations-leadership experience, ideally as a COO, VP of Operations, or director of operations in a professional-services or consulting firm.
- Fluency in finance operations for a services business: cash management, close, reporting, budgeting, and the metrics that drive a consultancy (utilization, realization, project margin, revenue per consultant).
- Strong HR and compliance grounding, including multi-state employment obligations, classification, and benefits.
- Experience standing up and running repeatable operating processes and reporting; comfort building structure where little exists.
- Sound judgment on IT, security, vendors, and risk for a small, cloud-based, distributed firm.
- Clear, plain communication and the ability to work within a defined governance and decision-rights model.
- Comfort operating in a firm with a founder-CEO, an elected President, and an emerging Governance Committee and trustee structure.
- Bonus: familiarity with data, ontology, knowledge-graph, or technology consulting, and with the professional-services delivery model.

## Success measures

- The firm's operations run smoothly and predictably; reporting is on time, consistent, and decision-ready.
- HR, finance, IT, and compliance obligations are met across the multi-state footprint, with issues caught early.
- Spending stays within budget and authority thresholds are respected; the President and CEO are not surprised.
- Accountability and authority are matched: the COO owns a clear lane and escalates by exception, not by default.
- Leadership is more durable and less dependent on any single person.

## Compensation and benefits

Competitive total cash compensation commensurate with a firm of this size (base plus an annual, profit-based bonus). Benefits include unlimited PTO, low-employee-cost health, dental, and vision insurance, an IRA with up to a 3% match, an individual training budget, device-purchase assistance, and student-loan repayment aid. Specific figures to be set per the firm's compensation review.
