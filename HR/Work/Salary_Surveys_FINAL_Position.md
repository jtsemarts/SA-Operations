## Bottom Line Up Front

After triangulating six independent sources — BLS actual-wage data (two release years), Salary.com, Payscale, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, and private-company survey commentary — and adjusting for Semantic Arts' competitive benefits, the defensible **total-cash (base + bonus)** position for these three overhead executive roles at a ~30-person, $5–10M-revenue specialist professional-services firm is:

| Role | 25th | 50th (median) | 75th | Planning point* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CEO** | $185K | $230K | $285K | **~$225K** |
| **President** | $170K | $210K | $265K | **~$205K** |
| **COO** | $150K | $185K | $240K | **~$185K** |

\*Planning point already reflects a ~5% downward adjustment for the firm's above-market benefits (see Section 3). Hierarchy preserved: CEO > President > COO.

These are **3–8% below the grounded memo** and **22–30% below the original aggregator pass** — the convergence reflects better sources, the newest BLS data, and the benefits offset you flagged.

## 1. Evidence Base — Multi-Source Triangulation

The sources disagree widely because they sample different populations. The discipline is to identify which population matches Semantic Arts (privately held, $5–10M, specialist, non-billable overhead roles) and discount the outliers.

### CEO

| Source | Figure | Population it reflects | Fit for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| BLS OEWS Chief Executives, May 2025 (national median) | **$192,160** | All employers, actual paid wages | High — best central anchor |
| BLS OEWS Chief Executives, May 2023 (median / mean) | $206,680 / $258,900 | All employers | High |
| BLS — Mgmt/Scientific/Technical Consulting (NAICS 541600), CE mean | $274,920 | Consulting industry, **mean** (skewed by big firms) | Medium — upper bound |
| ZipRecruiter "Small Business CEO" (25th–75th) | $81K–$121K | Micro-businesses / owner-operators | Low — too small |
| Chief Executive Group, private mid-market CEO median base | ~$325K–$336K | $50M+ private firms | Low — too large |

**Read:** Our firm sits above the micro-business aggregators and below mid-market. The BLS national median (~$192K–$207K) is the right center, with the consulting-industry mean (~$275K) as the ceiling for an aggressive position.

### President

| Source | Figure | Notes |

### COO

**Read:** The COO sources are the noisiest. Removing the enterprise outliers (Salary.com, Glassdoor), the small-firm-relevant cluster (ZipRecruiter + BLS G&OM) centers around **$150K–$200K** for a broad-scope COO at a firm this size.

## 2. Why the BLS Median Got *Lower*, Not Higher

The BLS Chief Executive median fell from **$206,680 (May 2023)** to **$192,160 (May 2025)**, while reported employment in the occupation jumped from ~211,000 to ~671,000. That roughly 3x jump signals a classification/scope change that pulled many more small-organization chief executives into the count — which *lowered* the median. For our purposes this is confirming evidence: when the data captures more small-firm CEOs (our peer set), the central figure lands near **$190K**, not the $300K+ the commercial "C-suite" aggregators imply.

## 3. Benefits / Total-Rewards Offset

Semantic Arts' benefits are above market (unlimited PTO, device-purchase assistance, low-employee-cost insurance). Because candidates evaluate **total reward**, a richer benefits package lets cash sit modestly below the market-median cash figure while keeping total reward at or above market. Quantifying the differentiated value (above a typical package):

| Component | Estimated annual value premium |
|---|---|
| Low-employee-cost health insurance (higher employer premium share) | ~$8K–$16K (single→family) |
| Unlimited PTO vs. a standard 15–20 day plan | ~$3K–$5K (notional) |
| Device-purchase assistance + other perks | ~$1K–$3K |
| **Total differentiated benefit premium** | **~$12K–$24K** |

Against a ~$200K–$230K cash package, that premium is roughly **5–10%**. Applying a conservative **~5%** discount to the market-median cash figure (reflected in the "planning point" column above) keeps total reward competitive while reducing cash outlay. Context: BLS ECEC shows benefits already average ~30% of total employer compensation cost across private industry; Semantic Arts sits above that average, which justifies the cash discount.

## 4. Positioning Guidance & Confidence

- **Confidence is now high** on the central figures: the BLS actual-wage anchor and the small-firm aggregator clusters independently converge near the planning points, and the enterprise outliers are explained and excluded.
- **CEO** ~$225K cash sits just above the BLS national median — appropriate for a specialist (higher-margin, higher-skill) firm, while staying well below mid-market.
- **President** ~$205K reflects the role's delivery/P&L weight, discounted for sub-1-year tenure; revisit at the 1–2 year mark.
- **COO** ~$185K reflects broad cross-functional scope (HR, marketing, sales, finance, office) at the top of the credible small-firm COO cluster.
- **Owner distributions** (if any executive is an owner) remain separate from these wage benchmarks — do not blend them into the comparison.
- **Geography:** national basis. A Fort Collins / Northern Colorado adjustment would trim these another few percent.
- **Aging:** BLS occupation detail is finalized to May 2025; the planning points already sit in 2026 dollars via the aggregator cross-checks, so no further inflation adjustment is needed.
- **For a formal board/comp-committee action**, these figures are well-supported for internal planning; a single purchased professional-services survey (Croner or Empsight) would add a named third-party citation.
