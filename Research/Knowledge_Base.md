# Research — Knowledge Base: Knowledge-Graph / Semantic Market & Competitors

**Maintained by:** Research · **Last updated:** July 3, 2026 · Living document

## 1. Overview & why it matters for Semantic Arts

Semantic Arts competes in enterprise ontology and knowledge-graph consulting and is a recognized thought leader in the data-centric movement. This knowledge base maps the market, the tailwinds, and the competitive field so the client-facing agents can position accurately.

## 2. Market size & key trends (2025–2026)

The category is small but growing fast and now has a powerful tailwind: **enterprise (and especially agentic) AI is driving demand for ontologies and knowledge graphs** as the shared-meaning layer AI needs. Market trackers put the agentic-AI-in-semantic-layer/knowledge-graph segment on a steep growth curve (roughly $0.85B in 2025 toward ~$3.2B by 2031 on one estimate — treat vendor market-sizing as directional) ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/agentic-artificial-intelligence-in-semantic-layer-and-knowledge-graph-market)).

The strongest signal is validation from top-tier advisors, all in 2025–2026: **BCG** ("Your AI Won't Scale Without a Shared Language"), **EY** ("Ontologies as the missing layer in enterprise AI"), **Forrester** ("Build Meaning Before Machines"), and **Gartner** elevating the semantic layer to essential infrastructure. The recurring thesis — data (not applications) as the enduring asset, ontology as the shared language, and the Finance-vs-Sales "revenue" definition problem — is precisely the argument SA has made for two decades.

## 3. Competitive landscape

Adjacent players and where SA fits:
- **Platform/vendor + services:** Stardog, Ontotext (GraphDB), Franz (AllegroGraph), TopQuadrant, metaphacts, data.world — tool-led, often with services attached.
- **KM/semantics consultancies:** Enterprise Knowledge (large, taxonomy/KM-led), Semantic Partners, and boutiques.
- **The big consultancies** (BCG, EY, Accenture, Deloitte) are now advising on the thesis, which grows the category and validates it, but they advise more than they build.

Semantic Arts' differentiation: it authored **gist** (a widely used upper ontology), it is vendor-neutral, and it actually builds real enterprise ontologies rather than only advising. Its scarcity is genuine — true senior ontologists are rare.

## 4. Application to Semantic Arts

The market has arrived at SA's long-held thesis, which is a positioning gift: five independent authorities now say the category is real. SA should differentiate on execution and gist against the advice-only consultancies, and on neutrality and depth against the tool-led vendors.
